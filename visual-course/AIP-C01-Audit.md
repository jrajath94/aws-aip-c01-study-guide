## Audit method

The content review follows nested layers: concept, prerequisite, mechanism, decision, causal trace, failure, experiment, and transfer.

The independent matrix rates all eight teaching criteria as substantive for all 98 skills.

A citation confirms traceability. It does not verify every sentence or predict a passing result.

The required primer resolves eight former prerequisite gaps.
The chapter dependency graph now follows concept dependencies rather than an artificial linear sequence.

## Practical limits

The console screenshots come from official AWS documentation.
They are static reference images. They are not evidence of executed labs in this account.
Each practical walkthrough states the action, reason, expected evidence, failure checks, and cleanup.
Console labels and supported features can change.
Some legacy Agents Classic images carry a current service limitation beside the figure.

The public AWS page confirms a 20-question official practice set with answer-choice feedback.
Its question bodies require enrollment and sign-in.
Those questions were not accessed or audited.
Search results labeled as exam dumps were not used.
All 250 extracted questions receive a semantic review against the saved sources.
The rationale review finds 85 supported items, 152 items that need qualification, eight incorrect rationales, and five uncertain rationales.
The course adds 87 specific corrections beside the affected questions.
Eight unsettled keys remain discussion cases and stay outside the timed mock.
This review does not live-verify every claim.
The course places correction notices beside inherited material.
Current official service documentation takes precedence over inherited absolutes.

## Readiness evidence

No result from this material can show whether you already know each concept.
Answer a new scenario without notes.
Explain the correct choice and each rejected option.
Predict the effect of one configuration change.
Use an independent official assessment before you treat familiarity as readiness.
AWS states that the exam guide is not a comprehensive list of exam content.
Two days of reading cannot certify production experience or guarantee a pass.
