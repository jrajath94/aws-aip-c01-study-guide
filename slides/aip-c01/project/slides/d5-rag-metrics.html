Task 5.1 · RAG metrics

Score the retriever and the generator apart

PartMetrics
RetrieverContext relevance and coverage. Did the system get the correct articles?
Retriever onlyPrecision at k and context relevance. No generation noise.
GeneratorFaithfulness. Are the claims supported by the chunks?
CitationsCitation precision. Do the citations point to the right text?

A drop in retriever-only scores points the regression at retrieval. Fine-tuning does not fix it.

Exam trap: A good final answer can hide weak retrieval. Measure both parts.
