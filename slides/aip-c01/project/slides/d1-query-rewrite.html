Task 1.5 · Query expansion and decomposition

Change the query before retrieval

TechniqueFit
Query expansionVocabulary gap. A Lambda step turns "refund" into the formal phrases the articles use.
Query decompositionMulti-hop question. Split it into sub-questions, retrieve for each, then merge.

Example sub-questions: supplier price increases after Q2, and EU shipping suppliers.

Exam trap: Decomposition answers multi-hop questions. Expansion answers vocabulary gaps. Pick by the failure.
