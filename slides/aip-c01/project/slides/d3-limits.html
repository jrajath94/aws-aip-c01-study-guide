Domain 3 · Honest limits

No control is absolute

Prompt attack detection finds known jailbreak and injection patterns. New attacks can pass.

Denied topics and content filters limit what the model discusses. They do not guarantee a clean answer.

Grounding checks reduce hallucination. They do not remove it.

Layers reduce risk. Testing and monitoring find what gets through.

Exam trap: Answers that say a control "prevents all prompt injection" or "eliminates hallucinations" are wrong.
