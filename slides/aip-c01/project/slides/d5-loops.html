Tasks 5.1 and 5.2 · Evaluation loops and A/B tests

Golden datasets, regression, canaries

PracticeWhat it does
Golden datasetTrusted prompts with known good answers. Run them again after every change.
Regression suiteReruns the golden set on each change to catch drift.
CanaryReleases a new version to a small share of traffic first.
A/B testCompares two variants side by side on live or sampled traffic.

Exam trap: A passing suite with new user failures means the golden dataset lacks those cases. Add them.
