Task 1.3 · Chunk tables and structure

Pick the chunk shape for the document shape

ContentChunking
Long structured document with tables and clausesHierarchical. Small child chunks match. Larger parent chunks give context.
Question and answer pairsOne chunk for each pair. Fixed small size or pre-chunked input.
Equations and figures with captionsCustom chunking with Lambda. Boundaries follow the structure.

Hierarchical chunks keep tables and clauses whole. Retrieval stays precise.

Exam trap: A table split at a chunk boundary loses its values. Each part can look correct alone.
