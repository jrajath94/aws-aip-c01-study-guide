Domain 1 answers one question

How do you get the correct model, the correct data, and the correct controls into a working system on AWS?

Climb the ladder. Use prompt engineering, then RAG, then agents, then fine-tuning. Stop at the cheapest step that works.

Prefer managed and serverless. The exam rewards the least operational overhead, unless a constraint forces custom work.

Map each constraint to one decision. Words such as "cite sources" point to RAG. "Take actions" points to agents.
