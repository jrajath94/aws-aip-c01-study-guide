Question types and scoring

Multiple choice

One correct response.

Three distractors.

Multiple response

Two or more correct responses out of five or more options.

You must select all correct responses to get credit.

Unanswered questions score as incorrect. Guessing has no penalty.

10 unscored questions are mixed in. The exam does not mark them.
