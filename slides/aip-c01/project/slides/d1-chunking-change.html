Task 1.4 and 1.5 · Retrieval symptoms and re-sync

Wrong chunks with wrong answers means retrieval failed

Chunks look relevant and answers are still wrong. Check the generation step first.

Split context destroys meaning across chunk boundaries.

Similarity is not relevance. Add reranking before generation.

After a re-sync, check what changed. Check the embedding model version and the chunking configuration.

Add a new data source with the new chunking setting. Then sync it to chunk and index its files.

Exam trap: Changing the model in the generator does not fix broken retrieval.
