Task 3.2 and 4.3 · Logs that hold prompts

Invocation logs hold the content. Protect them.

Invocation logs can hold prompts and completions. In a clinical setting, these can be PHI.

In a compliance environment, disable logging. Or encrypt logs with KMS and restrict access.

Model invocation logging to S3 with KMS encryption meets the encryption need.

S3 Object Lock gives tamper-evident retention. S3 Lifecycle expiration enforces the retention time.

Only model invocation logging captures prompt and response content. You must enable it. The guardrail trace records why a block happened.

Exam trap: Guardrail traces do not hold the full prompt. Enable invocation logging for content.
