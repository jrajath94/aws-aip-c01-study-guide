Task 3.2 and 4.3 · Logs that hold prompts

Invocation logs hold the content. Protect them.

Invocation logs can hold prompts and completions. In a clinical setting, these can be PHI.

In a compliance environment, disable logging. Or encrypt logs with KMS and restrict access.

Model invocation logging to S3 with KMS encryption meets the encryption need.

S3 Object Lock gives write-once-read-many (WORM) retention. S3 Lifecycle expiration deletes objects at the end of their lifetime.

Model invocation logging captures the full request and response. You must enable it. The guardrail trace records each assessment and action.

Exam trap: Invocation logs keep the original, unmodified request, even when a guardrail masks PII. Enable invocation logging for content.
