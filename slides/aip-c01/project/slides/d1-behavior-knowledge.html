Task 1.1 and 1.2 · Knowledge or behavior

Knowledge goes to retrieval. Behavior goes to weights.

NeedApproach
Private, current, or cited factsRAG with a Knowledge Base

Quality needs first. Then cost. Stop at the cheapest rung that works.

Exam trap: Fine-tuning a model to know changing documents is the classic wrong answer.
