Task 3.1 · Denied topics

Deny a subject area in plain language

A denied topic blocks a subject area. You describe the area in natural language.

Example: providing medical diagnoses.

The topic catches rephrased requests. A fixed word list does not.

Use a denied topic for a whole subject. Use a word filter for exact strings.

Exam trap: A word list cannot stop every phrasing of a subject. Pick the denied topic.
