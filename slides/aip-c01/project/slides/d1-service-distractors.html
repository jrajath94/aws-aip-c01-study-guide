Task 1.3 and 1.4 · Wrong services

Four services look right and are wrong

ServiceLooks right becauseWhy it is wrong
Amazon KendraEnterprise search over documentsNo generation step. Returns source documents only.
ElastiCacheFast in-memory readsEphemeral. Failover or eviction can lose embeddings.
Bedrock GuardrailsReads and checks document textChecks content against safety policies. Does not extract data.
Amazon TranslateChanges the language of textKeeps names, numbers, and identifiers. Does not hide PII.

Durable vector storage needs OpenSearch Serverless, Aurora with pgvector, or another durable store.

Exam trap: Read what each service stores and returns. Speed does not make a service durable.
