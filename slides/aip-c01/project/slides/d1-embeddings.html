Task 1.5 · Skill 1.5.2 · Embeddings

Embeddings turn meaning into vectors

Named embedding models: Amazon Titan Text Embeddings v2 and Cohere Embed. Titan offers 1024 or 256 dimensions.

Cohere Embed suits multilingual data. Set input_type to query for questions and to document for passages.

Choose by dimensions, cost, and domain fit. Test each model on your own golden set.

Embeddings are called with InvokeModel, not Converse.

Why keyword search is not enough: "reset my password" and "change my credentials" share few words. Vector search matches them. Keyword search misses them.
