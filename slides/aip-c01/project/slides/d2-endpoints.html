Task 2.2 · SageMaker endpoints

Pick the endpoint by traffic and job length

EndpointFit
Real-timeSelf-trained model with steady traffic. Autoscaling, custom containers, blue-green and rolling deployments.
Serverless InferenceIntermittent traffic. Scales to zero. Trade-off: cold start on the first request.
AsynchronousJobs from minutes to one hour. Queued. Result goes to S3, with an SNS notification.

Serverless Inference removes idle cost for traffic that comes and goes.

Exam trap: A real-time endpoint for a job that runs 45 minutes is the wrong pick. Use async.
