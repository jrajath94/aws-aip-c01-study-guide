The approach ladder

1. Prompt engineering

Start here. Cheapest step.

2. RAG

Documents change often, or answers must cite sources.

3. Agents

The model must take actions, such as calling tools.

4. Fine-tuning

Stable behavior and a large, repeated task volume.

Stop at the cheapest step that works.

Exam trap: "Fine-tune the model on company documents" is wrong when the documents update regularly. Use RAG.
