Task 1.5 · Skill 1.5.1 · Chunking

Choose a chunking strategy by document type

StrategyUse whenNote
Fixed-sizeFAQs. Short, self-contained text. Simple text.Lambda can implement it. Q and A pairs: one chunk per pair.
HierarchicalManuals, legal contracts, long documents with headings.Small child chunks match. Parent chunks give context. Keeps tables whole.
SemanticQuality matters more than cost.Splits on meaning. Costs more to process.
Custom with LambdaEquations, figures with captions, structured layouts.Boundaries follow the document structure.

The exam maps the document type to the strategy. Match the structure first.

Exam trap: Do not pick a strategy by habit. A table split across chunks loses its values.
